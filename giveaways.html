How to Redeem Your Promo Code

1

Claim Your Code

Click "Claim Free Promo Code" on any active watch face above to reveal your single-use Google Play code.

2

Redeem on Google Play

Tap "Redeem on Google Play" or open the Google Play Store app → Tap your profile avatar → Payments & subscriptions → Redeem code.

3

Install on Your Watch

Once redeemed, the watch face is permanently tied to your Google account. Select your Wear OS watch device during install to download it directly to your smartwatch.
