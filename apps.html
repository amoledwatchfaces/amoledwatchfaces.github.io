Wear OS Apps

Enhance your watch (faces). I created these apps to give you more options for customizing watch faces. Since app development takes time, not all wearable apps are free.

Weather Complications Paid

Standalone Weather & Astro Data for Wear OS. Everything runs directly on your device — no accounts, no tracking, and direct access to trusted forecast providers.

Privacy Policy

Phone Battery Complication Open Source Free

Sync and display your phone's battery percentage and charging state directly on your watch face. Everything communicates locally and directly between your devices — no accounts, no tracking, and no external servers.

Privacy Policy GitHub

Complications Suite Open Source Free

Add custom complications to your Wear OS watch face. Features world clocks, water trackers, custom dates/text, sunrise & sunset indicators, moon phases, timers, cryptocurrency price tickers, and settings shortcuts running local-first.

Privacy Policy GitHub

Favorite Apps Tile Paid

Create custom tile launchers to quickly access your favorite apps on Wear OS. Support for multiple tiles allows you to organize your watch applications into custom category launchers.

Privacy Policy

Health Plugin Paid

Supplement your watch face with custom steps, heart rate, active calories, distance, and floors climbed complications powered entirely locally by Google Wear Health Services.

Privacy Policy

Photo Complication Paid

Display custom photos or shuffle dynamic collections of local images directly inside watch face complications. Fully supports large image, small image, and background complications.

Privacy Policy
