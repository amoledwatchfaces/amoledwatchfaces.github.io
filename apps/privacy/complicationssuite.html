Complications Suite

Custom Date, World Clock, Water Tracker, Sunrise & Moon Phases, Timers & Crypto Prices

Last Updated: August 26, 2026

Privacy Ethos

This app is designed as a fully local helper utility to add custom complications to your Wear OS watch face, including world clocks, water intake trackers, custom dates, sunrise & sunset data, moon phases, timers, custom text, cryptocurrency prices (Bitcoin/Ethereum), and settings shortcuts. We believe your information belongs to you. Our core principles are:

  • No Advertising: We do not include any ad SDKs or tracking pixels.
  • No External Trackers: This application does not integrate Firebase, Google Analytics, or any other third-party crash reporting/tracking SDKs.
  • No User Profiles: We do not collect names, emails, or hardware identifiers (IMEI/Serial).
  • Local-First Processing: All timer configurations, app launcher shortcuts, astronomical calculations, and device setting toggles are processed entirely locally on your watch. Cryptocurrency prices are fetched directly from public APIs without sending any user metrics.

Data Access & Permissions

The following permissions and services are used strictly to power specific complication types on your watch face:

Permission / Service Usage & Purpose Feature Powered
ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION Used to fetch your approximate location to calculate sun/moon position, sunrise/sunset, and astronomical data. Location is never stored or transmitted. Moon Phase & Sun Position complications.
QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES Retrieves the list of installed applications on your watch so you can select and launch them directly from your watch face. App Launcher & Activity Shortcut complications.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM & USE_EXACT_ALARM Schedules precise timer updates to ensure notifications and alerts trigger at the correct second. Timer complication.
VIBRATE Triggers physical watch vibration when a scheduled timer expires. Timer complication alerts.
CHANGE_WIFI_STATE Allows the application to toggle or adjust the Wi-Fi status on the device. Wi-Fi Toggle settings shortcut.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Posts alerts or banners on the watch to inform you when a timer has finished. Timer complication alerts.
INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Used to verify network status, perform basic network state actions for shortcuts, and connect to public API endpoints to retrieve real-time cryptocurrency prices. No personal data is ever transmitted or stored. Network shortcuts & Crypto price complications.

Data Sharing & External Services

No third-party SDKs (such as Firebase Crashlytics) or diagnostic systems are present in the app. There is no background tracking or diagnostic sharing of any kind.

Data Storage & Retention

  • Local Storage: Your complication preferences, custom text configurations, and timer durations are stored privately on your watch using standard Android system preferences.
  • Uninstall: All settings and cached data are permanently deleted by the Android system when you uninstall the app from your watch.

No Data Sale

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to third parties. We do not track your device behavior or usage patterns.

User Rights & Control

You have full control over the permissions and settings directly from your watch:

  • Revoke Location: You can disable location access at any time via Settings > Apps > Permissions on your watch, which will disable location-based moon/sun tracking but keep all other complications fully functional.

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Our services are intended for a general audience.
