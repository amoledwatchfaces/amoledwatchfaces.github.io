Photo Complication

Display your favorite custom photos or shuffle dynamic image collections directly inside watch face complications

Last Updated: August 27, 2026

Privacy Ethos

This app is designed as a local media helper utility that allows you to render static photos or rotate collections of pictures inside watch face complications on Wear OS. We believe your information belongs to you. Our core principles are:

  • No Advertising: We do not include any ad SDKs or tracking pixels.
  • No External Trackers: This application does not integrate Firebase, Google Analytics, or any other third-party crash reporting/tracking SDKs.
  • No User Profiles: We do not collect names, emails, or hardware identifiers (IMEI/Serial).
  • Local-First Processing: Your image selection, shuffling parameters, and local file URIs are processed and stored entirely locally on your watch. We do not transmit your photos or metadata to any external server.

Data Access & Permissions

The following permissions are used strictly to select and display images inside watch face complications:

Permission / Service Usage & Purpose Feature Powered
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / READ_MEDIA_IMAGES Used to access the device's local gallery or storage to select and display custom image files in complications. Photo picking & complication rendering.

Data Sharing & External Services

No third-party SDKs (such as Firebase Crashlytics) or diagnostic systems are present in the app. There is no background tracking or diagnostic sharing of any kind.

Data Storage & Retention

  • Local Storage: Your picked image files (or their local references) and configuration settings are stored locally on your watch using standard system storage.
  • Uninstall: All references and settings are permanently deleted by the Android system when you uninstall the app from your watch.

No Data Sale

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to third parties. We do not track your device behavior or usage patterns.
