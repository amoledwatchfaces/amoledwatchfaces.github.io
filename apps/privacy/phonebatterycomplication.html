Phone Battery Complication for Wear OS

Sync Battery Levels Between Your Phone & Watch

Last Updated: August 25, 2026

Privacy Ethos

This app is designed as a direct, helper utility to display your phone's battery level on your watch face. We believe your device status is personal. Our core principles are:

  • No Advertising: We do not include any ad SDKs or tracking pixels.
  • No Location Tracking: We do not request, access, or track your location coordinates.
  • No User Profiles: We do not collect names, emails, or hardware identifiers (IMEI/Serial).
  • Local & Direct Sync: Battery level communication happens directly between your phone and watch using Google Play Services Wearable APIs. No proprietary cloud servers are used to store or route this data.

Data Access & Permissions

The following permissions and services are used to synchronize and display battery information:

Permission/Service Usage & Purpose Feature Powered
Google Play Services (Wearable API) Used to transfer battery levels and charging states between the phone and watch. Real-time battery synchronization.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Allows the application to re-initialize battery level sync services when your device boots up. Automatic background syncing.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE Ensures background battery monitoring tasks can run reliably. Complication status updates.

Data Sharing & External Services

All core synchronization operates entirely between your own paired devices. The only external service present in the app is optional diagnostics:

Crash Reporting & Diagnostics (Optional)

To help improve application stability and diagnose bugs, the app contains optional crash reporting integrations:

Firebase Crashlytics (Google) Privacy Policy
Disabled by default. If you explicitly opt-in to crash reporting in the app settings, Crashlytics will transmit anonymous crash logs, stack traces, and basic device characteristics (such as OS version and device model) to help us identify and resolve bugs. No personally identifiable information (PII) or location data is ever included in these reports.
Google Play Data Safety Declarations:
  • Data Types: Crash logs & Device or other IDs.
  • Collected, Shared, or both? Collected only (not Shared, as Google/Firebase processes this data solely on our behalf).
  • Ephemeral Processing: No (Firebase stores crash logs and installation IDs on its servers for analysis).
  • User Choice: Users can choose whether this data is collected (collection is completely optional and controlled by an in-app toggle).
  • Purpose of Collection: App functionality and/or Analytics.

Data Storage & Retention

  • Local Storage: Only the current battery percentage and charging state are temporarily cached locally on your device to feed the watch complication.
  • Retention: Synced values are overwritten immediately whenever a new battery update is received. No historical battery level history is stored.
  • Uninstall: All settings and cache are immediately deleted by the system when you uninstall the app from your device.

No Data Sale

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to third parties. We do not track your device behavior or usage patterns.

User Rights & Control

You have full control over the sync status and options:

  • Stop Sync: You can pause synchronization or uninstall the companion app at any time to halt communication.
  • Crash Reporting Opt-out: You can enable or disable crash reporting at any time via the application settings on your device.

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Our services are intended for a general audience.
