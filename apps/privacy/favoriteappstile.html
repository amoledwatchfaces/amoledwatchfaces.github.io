Last Updated: August 27, 2026

Privacy Ethos

This app is designed as a local launcher utility allowing you to quickly launch your selected watch applications from custom tiles. We believe your information belongs to you. Our core principles are:

  • No Advertising: We do not include any ad SDKs or tracking pixels.
  • No External Trackers: This application does not integrate Firebase, Google Analytics, or any other third-party crash reporting/tracking SDKs.
  • No User Profiles: We do not collect names, emails, or hardware identifiers (IMEI/Serial).
  • Local-First Processing: All tile configurations and app shortcuts are stored and processed entirely locally on your watch.

Data Access & Permissions

The following permission is used strictly to power specific tile launcher shortcuts on your watch:

Permission / Service Usage & Purpose Feature Powered
QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES Retrieves the list of installed applications on your watch so you can select and launch them directly from custom tiles. App selection list & tile launcher shortcuts.

Data Sharing & External Services

No third-party SDKs (such as Firebase Crashlytics) or diagnostic systems are present in the app. There is no background tracking or diagnostic sharing of any kind.

Data Storage & Retention

  • Local Storage: Your tile app shortcuts are stored privately on your watch using standard Android system preferences.
  • Uninstall: All configurations are permanently deleted by the Android system when you uninstall the app from your watch.

No Data Sale

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to third parties. We do not track your device behavior or usage patterns.
