Privacy Policy

Wear OS Watch Faces by amoledwatchfaces

Last Updated: August 29, 2026

Overview & Core Principles

At amoledwatchfaces, we respect your privacy. Our Wear OS watch faces are built with a privacy-by-design approach. We do not collect, store, share, or sell any of your personal data.

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, IP addresses, or device serial numbers.
  • No Tracking or Analytics: Our watch faces do not include third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or telemetry frameworks.
  • 100% Local Execution: All watch faces run entirely on your watch device using Google's native Watch Face Format (WFF).

Device Permissions & Sensor Data

Depending on the features and complications you choose to display, a watch face may request specific system permissions on your Wear OS device:

Sensor Data (Heart Rate & Health)

If a watch face displays body sensor metrics (e.g. heart rate or step count), it accesses these values locally in real time via the standard Wear OS / Health Services APIs. This information is processed exclusively on your device to display the complication value and is never recorded, transmitted, or shared externally.

Complication Data

Watch faces support user-selected complication slots (such as calendar events, weather, world clock, or battery status). All complication data is supplied directly by your chosen system complication providers and Wear OS. The watch face merely renders the provided data on the screen.

Third-Party Services

Our watch faces do not integrate with any external cloud servers or third-party data brokers. Standard purchases, downloads, and app updates are managed directly and securely through Google Play, subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

Our watch faces do not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements or regulatory updates. Any changes will be posted directly on this page with an updated revision date.
