Privacy Policy

Wear OS Watch Faces, Apps, and Website by amoledwatchfaces™

Last Updated: September 4, 2026

Overview & Core Principles

At amoledwatchfaces™ (operated by independent developer Tomáš Marcinčin), we are deeply committed to user privacy. Whether you are using our Wear OS watch faces, Android companion apps, or browsing our website, we operate with a strict privacy-by-design philosophy.

  • No User Profiling or Selling Data: We do not track you across the web, profile your behavior, or sell any personal data to third parties or brokers.
  • No Analytics or Ad Trackers: We do not load Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or commercial advertising SDKs.
  • Minimal Data Processing: We only process the absolute minimum data required to deliver requested services (such as answering your support emails or delivering opt-in notifications).

1. Wear OS Watch Faces & Applications

Our Wear OS watch faces and Android apps are designed for local, offline execution directly on your watch device:

Local Execution & Permissions

All watch faces run natively using Google's declarative Watch Face Format (WFF) without external network calls or telemetry frameworks.

  • Body Sensor Data (Heart Rate & Health): If a watch face displays heart rate or step counts, it queries the local Wear OS / Health Services APIs in real time. This information never leaves your device and is never recorded or transmitted externally.
  • Complications: Watch faces render complications provided directly by your selected system complication providers and Wear OS. The watch face merely displays the data on screen.
  • Google Play Purchases & Updates: App downloads, license verification, and updates are handled securely and exclusively by Google Play under the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Website Privacy Policy (amoledwatchfaces.com)

This section describes how data is handled when you visit our website:

Infrastructure, Hosting & CDN

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages and protected by Cloudflare as a content delivery network (CDN) and reverse proxy.

When you visit our site, Cloudflare and GitHub automatically process standard server log data (such as your IP address, browser user-agent, operating system, and request timestamps) strictly for delivering web pages, optimizing speed, and defending against DDoS attacks or malicious traffic. This processing is based on our legitimate interest under Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR to provide a secure and stable website.

Customer Support & Contact Inquiries

When you submit an inquiry through our Contact Form or email us directly at support@amoledwatchfaces.com:

  • Data Collected: Your name, email address, topic, and message content.
  • Service Provider: The contact form securely forwards your message to us using Web3Forms.
  • Purpose & Legal Basis: We use this information solely to review, respond to, and resolve your support inquiries, bug reports, or BOGO claims. The legal basis for this processing is GDPR Article 6(1)(b) (taking steps at the request of the user prior to or in fulfillment of a service) and Article 6(1)(f) (legitimate interest in providing responsive customer support).
  • Retention: Support correspondence is retained only as long as necessary to resolve your request and is deleted upon request.

Web Push Notifications

Our website offers optional Web Push Notifications to alert you about new watch face releases, updates, and promotions.

  • Consent-Based (Opt-In): Notifications are entirely optional. A push token is only created if you voluntarily click the notification bell icon and explicitly grant permission in your browser prompt (GDPR Article 6(1)(a) Consent).
  • Technology: We use Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM). An anonymous, randomly generated device registration token is created and stored in your browser's local storage. No names, emails, or personal identities are attached to this token.
  • Revoking Consent: You can withdraw consent and disable notifications at any time simply by clicking the bell icon on the website or changing your browser notification permissions. When disabled, your token is immediately deleted from local storage and unsubscribed.

Cookies & Local Storage

We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party behavioral analytics cookies.

We use your browser's native localStorage strictly for functional, non-tracking site preferences:

  • theme: Remembers your dark or light mode preference.
  • snowDisabled: Remembers whether you have paused the seasonal particle animation.
  • awf_giveaway_code_*: Stores your claimed giveaway promo code in your browser so you don't lose it before redeeming it on Google Play.
  • fcm_token & notifications_enabled: Stores your notification status if you explicitly opted into announcements.

Because these storage items are strictly necessary for functional UI and user-requested features and do not track you across other websites, a consent banner is not required under the EU ePrivacy Directive.

3. Your Rights Under GDPR (EU / EEA Users)

If you reside in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have specific statutory rights regarding your personal data under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right of Access (Art. 15 GDPR): You have the right to request confirmation of whether we hold personal data concerning you (e.g. past support emails) and obtain a copy.
  • Right to Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR): You have the right to request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Right to Erasure (Art. 17 GDPR): You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data (the "Right to be Forgotten"), including past support messages or stored notification tokens.
  • Right to Restriction of Processing (Art. 18 GDPR): You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your data under certain conditions.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent (Art. 7(3) GDPR): Where processing is based on consent (such as push notifications), you may withdraw your consent at any time without penalty.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: You have the right to file a complaint with a competent Data Protection Authority in your EU member state.

To exercise any of these rights, simply email us at support@amoledwatchfaces.com. We will respond promptly within the statutory timeline.

4. Children's Privacy

Our services, watch faces, and website are not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.
