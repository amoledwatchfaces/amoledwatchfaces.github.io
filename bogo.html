Buy One, Get One (BOGO)

Buy a watch face or app from our portfolio and receive another watch face of your choice for free.

How It Works

1

Buy a Watch Face or App

Buy a watch face or an app from our Google Play Store portfolio.

2

Choose Your Free Watch Face

Pick a watch face of equal or lesser price from our portfolio.

3

Email Your Receipt

Email the purchase receipt and the chosen watch face name to support@amoledwatchfaces.com.

And that's it! You'll receive a promo code for the selected watch face within three days.

Promotion Terms & Disclaimer

Only purchase receipts not older than 10 days are valid. The most recent watch face release is not eligible to be received as part of the BOGO promotion.
